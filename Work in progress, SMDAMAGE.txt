## Units audit, 2026-06-29

### Summary of findings
The code is internally consistent in using megatons of carbon (MtC) throughout for the carbon emitter/remover decision variables and their bid prices.
The price series saved to smdamage_solutions.db via the Vpt constraint duals are in $M/MtC, i.e., dollars per ton of carbon. They are NOT per ton of CO2. The existing commented-out reminder in SMDAMAGE revenue neutral.py ("Reminder: convert $/ton C to $/ton CO2") confirms this is a known, unresolved presentation gap.

One genuine numerical issue was found: Agriculture bid prices in Agriculture_bids.csv are in $/MtC (the header says $US2022), but the quantity column header says "Mgtons C" rather than "MtC". The "M" in "Mgtons" is ambiguous — Mtons (megatons) and "Gtons" (gigatons) are contradictory prefixes.
The data values (~87–513 Mgtons C) match the scale of MtC (megatons of carbon) not gigatons, so the actual numbers appear to be megatons. The header label "Mgtons C" is misleading — it should read "MtC".

### Detailed trace: carbon emitter/remover decision variable units

**Input data (smdamage_data.db)**
- MtC_bid_steps.csv: header is "Marginal $2020/ton C, Mtons C". Prices in $/ton C, quantities in megatons of carbon. These are scaled to global 2022 FFI baseline of 10,232 MtC.
- Agriculture_bids.csv: header is "$US2022, Mgtons C". Prices in $/MtC (negative for removers), quantities nominally in "Mgtons C" but values are consistent with MtC scale (~87–513 units). Label is ambiguous ("Mgtons C" could be read as millions of gigtons; intended meaning is megatons).
- Seaweed_bids.csv: header is "Million/million ha seaweed, Million tons carbon". Prices in $million/million ha, quantities in million tons carbon (= MtC). Consistent.
- Forestry_bid_steps.csv: bid prices in $/hectare (NPV), quantities in plantable million hectares/year. Forestry bidder units in DB are 'mhectares'. Carbon removal is tracked separately in forestry_removal (tons/hectare/year). Consistent.
- CH4_bid_steps.csv: header says "Bid price $/MtonN20, Bid qty Mton N20" — note the column header says "N20" (number 20) instead of "N2O" in both the CH4 and N2O files. The CH4 file appears to contain CH4 data (quantities are ~0.3–90 MtCH4) and the N2O file appears to contain N2O data (~0.1–1.6 MtN2O). The header label "N20" vs. "N2O" is a typo but appears to not affect data loading (the loader reads by column position, not header name).
- C2F6_CF4_HFC125_HFC134a_HFC143a_SF6_bidsteps.csv: prices in $/kt, quantities in kt/year. Stored with units='kt' in DB. Correctly divided by 1000 in read_bids() to convert to $M/kt for objective function consistency.

**create_database.py**
- 'Carbon' bidder: units = 'mtC', hector_name = 'ffi_emissions'.
- 'Agriculture', 'Seaweed': units = 'mtC', hector_name = 'ffi_emissions'. Both removers share the same Hector channel as Carbon (ffi), which is correct for CO2-equivalent removal via the terrestrial carbon cycle.
- F-gases (C2F6, CF4, etc.): units = 'kt', hector_name = '{chemical}_emissions'. Correct.

**Warming factor pulse (Calibrated_pulses_by_chemical_2025.txt)**
- ffi_emissions: emission_factor = 1, units = GtC/yr. Pulse values are in degrees C per GtC pulse.
- Conversion in get_warming_effects(): Wpt_dict[('Carbon', t0)] = Pulse['ffi'][t0+1] * 1000.0 / Pulse['ffi'][0] / 1000.0. The two 1000 factors cancel: numerator 1000 converts degrees to thousandths-of-a-degree; denominator 1000 converts GtC to MtC. Net: Wpt in thousandths-of-a-degree per MtC. Correct.
- luc_emissions: emission_factor = 1.1955, units = GtC/yr. Treated identically to ffi for Agriculture when is_removal_luc = True. Correct.
- Non-CO2 gases (CH4, N2O, F-gases): Wpt_dict[p,t0] = Pulse[p][t0+1] * 1000.0 / Pulse[p][0]. No additional /1000 because the chemical bid quantities are already in the native Hector units (MtCH4, MtN2O, kt). Consistent.

**Objective function (read_bids() in SMDAMAGE revenue neutral.py)**
- For 'mtC' bidders: Bapt[a,p,t] = price_per_unit * discount_rate(t). Price is in $/ton C (from CSV); result is in $M/MtC after the 1.23 CPI inflation multiplier applied at solve time. So the objective coefficients are in $M/MtC discounted. Consistent with quantities (MtC).
- For 'kt' bidders (F-gases): Bapt[a,p,t] = price_per_unit * discount_rate(t) / 1000. Price is in $/ton (kt basis), so /1000 converts to $M/kt. Correct.
- For 'mhectares' bidders (forestry): price is NPV in $/hectare from Busch; converted to $M/mhectare in the forestry bid curve. Consistent.
- Objective comment in solver: "All objective function coefficients should be millions of dollars." Confirmed true for Carbon and removers.

**Decision variable vpt and dual price Vpt(p,t)**
- vpt[p,t] is the total quantity of bidder p accepted in year t.
- For Carbon: vpt['Carbon', t] is in MtC (megatons of carbon, not CO2).
- The constraint dual pi for Vpt(Carbon,t) is the Lagrange multiplier on the quantity balance equation: vpt(p,t) = sum of qapt. Its economic interpretation is the marginal value (shadow price) of one additional unit of the pollutant in year t.
- Units of the dual: The dual has units of [objective units] / [constraint RHS units] = $M/MtC. This equals $1 per ton of carbon (since $M/MtC = $1,000,000 / 1,000,000 tonnes = $1/tonne).
- So: **Vpt(Carbon,t).pi is in $/ton of carbon, NOT $/ton of CO2**.
- To convert to $/ton CO2, multiply by 12/44 = 0.2727 (the mass ratio of carbon to CO2).
- The commented-out line in SMDAMAGE revenue neutral.py: "Reminder: convert $/ton C to $/ton CO2. Temp in 2125 is..." confirms this conversion is needed for publication but has not been applied in the output plots.

**Price_trajectory_with_full_commitment_tau_1_6 in plotting_utils.py**
- Calls get_vpt_dual_series_by_scenario_id(..., 'Carbon') which reads Vpt(Carbon,year).pi from constraint_duals.
- The negation [-series[year]] applied in the plot call: the Vpt(Carbon,t) row is the constraint vpt(p,t) == sum(qapt), so for emitters whose qapt enters the objective with a positive coefficient, the LP standard convention makes pi < 0 (the dual is negative because relaxing the upper bound on emissions reduces cost). The negation converts this to a positive carbon price display. Economically correct.
- Y-axis label is "Carbon price". Should note the unit: $/ton C (not $/ton CO2).

**Temperature series stored in smdamage_solutions.db**
- Source field "SMDAMAGE actual temp calibrated" and temperature_series values: stored as thousandths of a degree C (= initial_temperature + temperatureChange[t].varValue).
Initial_temperature (e.g. 1108.916 or 981.858) is in thousandths of degrees C.
Confirmed by units field 'thousandths_C' in scenario_series and by /1000 division in all plotting functions. Consistent.
- "SMDAMAGE Carbon calibrated" series: stores vpt['Carbon',t].varValue in MtC. The plotting function divides by 1000 to display in gigatons. Correct.
- "SMDAMAGE yearly revenue calibrated" series: stores yearlyrevenue[t] = sum(-vpt[p,t] * Vpt.pi) in $M. Plotting divides by 1000 to display in $billions. Correct.

### Issues found

1. **Agriculture_bids.csv header ambiguity** (low severity): The quantity column header "Mgtons C" is misleading. Values are consistent with MtC (megatons of carbon). Recommend changing to "MtC" for clarity.

2. **CH4_bid_steps.csv and N2O_bid_steps.csv headers typo** (cosmetic): Both files say "MtonN20" and "Mton N20" instead of "MtCH4/MtN2O". Does not affect the model because column position is used, not header text, but it is confusing.

3. **Price plots are in $/ton C, not $/ton CO2** (presentation gap, confirmed known): All Vpt dual prices for Carbon, Agriculture, and Seaweed are in $/ton of carbon. For publication, divide by 12/44 ≈ 0.2727 (equivalently multiply by 44/12 ≈ 3.667) to convert to $/ton CO2. The conversion factor is already noted in data_sources.txt (CO2->C conversion factor 3.66406 cited in the plan for prepare_inputs.py). Price_trajectory_with_full_commitment_tau_1_6 inherits this gap.

4. **Seaweed and Agriculture share hector_name = 'ffi_emissions'** (design note, not a bug): Both removers are treated as carbon equivalent to fossil fuel removal via the atmosphere. This is a modeling assumption, not a coding error, but worth documenting for reviewers.

5. **No unit inconsistency found in Wpt computation**: The double application of ×1000 and ÷1000 in get_warming_effects() (scaleCelsius=1000 for thousandths-of-a-degree, divided by 1000 to convert GtC to MtC) is internally consistent.

6. **Forestry Wpt double-scaling risk** (worth checking): The forestry convolution applies `tc * ag_wpt_arr * scaleCelsius / 1000.0` where tc is in tons/hectare/year and ag_wpt_arr is already in thousandths-of-a-degree per MtC. The /1000 converts tons/hectare to Mtons/hectare correctly.
But since vpt[tree,t] is in mhectares, the product vpt * tc gives mhectares * tons/ha/yr = Mtons/yr, which is MtC/yr. Multiplying by Wpt (thousandths-C per MtC) then gives thousandths-C per mhectare planted. Appears correct.
