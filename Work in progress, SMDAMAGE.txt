### Step 2 — Import into smdamage_data.db (load_forestry_from_busch_sqlite)
  price_per_unit = -npv_max_per_ha = -$63.6543/ha  (negated for remover convention)
  quantity_units = step_area / 1,000,000 = 0.165191 Mha
  discount_rate = discount_rate_00 / 1000 = 30 / 1000 = 0.03  ✓
  available_area_mhectares (forestry_bidder_metadata) = 5.5061 Mha  ✓
  Total quantity across all bid steps = 5.451 Mha (≈ 99% of total area; top 1% omitted per Busch bucket design).
  Import is correct and lossless.

### Step 3 — Unit consistency
  price_per_unit is in $/ha.
  quantity_units is in Mha.
  Revenue = price × qty = ($/ha) × Mha = ($/ha) × (10^6 ha) = $10^6 = $1M.
  So 1 $/ha = 1 $M/Mha.  The objective is in $M; units are CONSISTENT throughout.

### Step 4 — read_bids (SMDAMAGE revenue neutral.py)
  Bids for bidder 'Forestry_r100_c03' are selected by discount_rate_base = 0.03 (scenario parameter).
  The DB stores one bid curve per discount rate, so only the 3% bids are loaded.
  Bapt[step, bidder, t] = price_per_unit * scenario.discount_rate(t − StartYear)
    where scenario.discount_rate(lag) = 1 / (1.03^lag)  (time-discounting the contract payment).
  At t=2034 (lag=9): Bapt = -63.6543 * 1/1.03^9 = -48.79 $/ha.
  Uapt[step, bidder, t] = quantity_units / periodsPerYear = 0.165191 Mha (unchanged).
  No /1000 divisor is applied (that divisor only applies to kt-denominated bidders like C2F6).

### Step 5 — Objective function (Solve_SMDAMAGE)
  Objective coefficient = inflate_2020_to_2025 × Bapt = 1.03^5 × (-48.79) = 1.1593 × (-48.79) = -56.56 $M/Mha.
  This is the present-value contribution of planting 1 Mha at t=2034 in the objective (negative for a remover).
  The revenue earned (objective decrease) is 56.56 $M per Mha, i.e., $56.56/ha.
  Equivalently: the bid price -63.65 $/ha, discounted 9 years at 3% and inflated 5 years from 2020→2025.

### Step 6 — Dual prices (scenario 10, Figs3-5, tau=2.0, temp0=1097.123, Wpt fitted)
  Vpt(Forestry_r100_c03, t=2025..2033).pi = -215.61 $/ha  (planting here is not optimal; very high opportunity cost)
  Vpt(Forestry_r100_c03, t=2034).pi = +63.76 $/ha   ← active planting year; pi ≈ undiscounted bid price
  Vpt(Forestry_r100_c03, t=2064).pi = +31.89 $/ha   ← the figure the user noticed
  Vpt(Forestry_r100_c03, t=2073..2085).pi ≈ -200 $/ha  (land constraint binding; temperature critical)

### Explanation of the $31.89/ha figure
  $31.89/ha at t=2064 is the Vpt dual price for PLANTING at year 2064 — not the price for the 2034 contracts.
  It is lower than 63.65 because:
    (a) time discounting: 1/1.03^30 ≈ 0.41 relative to 2034, and
    (b) the temperature constraint shadow prices change over time.
  The market-clearing price for the ACTIVE planting year (t=2034) is $63.76/ha, which is just above the
  minimum bid of $63.65/ha. This confirms the auction is pricing forestry correctly at the margin.

### Conclusion
  No unit errors found in the bid pipeline. The bids, units, discount conversion, and objective coefficients
  are all internally consistent. The $31.89/ha figure is the shadow price of planting at t=2064, not the
  clearing price for the cheapest bid step. The clearing price at the active planting year (t=2034) is $63.76/ha,
  consistent with the $63.65 lowest bid after a small temperature-benefit premium.
